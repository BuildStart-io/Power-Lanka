# Service Descriptions

## Adobe Creative Cloud (with Nano Banana)

**Service Overview:**
Premium Adobe Creative Cloud subscription service provided by "Nano Banana".

**Benefit Highlights:**

1. **Full Warranty Provided**: Every subscription comes with a complete warranty for the duration of the plan.
2. **Own Email Access**: Customers can use their own personal email address for the subscription.
3. **No Shared Accounts**: Subscriptions are private and not shared with other users.

## Facebook Personal Account Verification (Meta Verified)

**Service Overview:**
This service is referred to when customers ask for 'Rs.760 membership' or 'Meta Verification'. It allows users to verify their personal Facebook profiles.

**Pricing Structure:**

* **Initial setup fee:** LKR 5000 (one-time payment).
* **First month:** Meta Verification is provided **FREE** of charge.
* **Renewal (2nd month onwards):** Customer pays approximately LKR 760 per month directly to Meta.

**Eligibility:**

* Available **ONLY** for Facebook Personal Accounts.
* **NOT** supported for WhatsApp or Facebook Pages.

**Requirements:**

* **KYC Verification:** Customer account must be KYC verified.
* **Documents:** Passport, Driving License, or National ID is required.
* **Assistance:**
  * If the customer has not completed KYC, we can assist **free of charge**.
  * Customers can also do it themselves if they prefer.
  * If unsure, advise customers to check YouTube tutorials.

**Support:**

* Lifetime support is provided for this service.
